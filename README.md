# ITACHI UCHIHA — 万華鏡写輪眼 · 赤い月

An immersive, cinematic web experience dedicated to **Itachi Uchiha (うちはイタチ)**.

## Features
- **Act I — 覚醒 (Awaken)**: Interactive scroll-scrubbed gaze awakening under the blood moon.
- **Act II — 漆黒の烏 (Crows Particle Cloud)**: 3D interactive particle flock of crows reassembling into Itachi's silhouette.
- **Act IV — 烏分身 (Karasu Bunshin)**: 4K UHD artwork with autonomous flocking crows and swirling feathers physics engine, dual Mangekyo Sharingan eye flares, and click dispersion shockwave.
- **Act V — 月読 (Tsukuyomi)**: Interactive Tsukuyomi eye tracking.
- **Act VI — 哀しき宿命 (Legacy)**: Ghost cursor Amaterasu burn-through reveal of the farewell scene with Sasuke.
- **Soundtrack Engine**: Official Naruto Shippuden Akatsuki theme with top-navigation audio HUD and equalizer.
